### Google DeepMind launches AlphaGenome 2 to predict mutation effects from 100M+ cell profiles

Google DeepMind has launched AlphaGenome 2, an AI model trained on more than 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues to predict how genetic mutations affect specific cell types and states. Available for non-commercial research, it reportedly identifies nearly twice as many variants that alter gene expression as its predecessor and can make predictions for new cell types without retraining. DeepMind says the model could help link genetic variants to cellular effects and disease, potentially aiding treatment research.

### Can a Linear Program Do Weight-Space Programming?

MIT CSAIL’s “Sparks of Weight-Space Programming” reports that GPT-6 Astra can reason directly over neural-network weights, including editing Qwen3.5-0.8B to reduce thinking loops—changing 0.016% of weights and raising MATH-500 accuracy from 7.9% to 48.1% and IFEval from 21% to 62%. The article argues Astra’s weight-edit search was effectively solving a linear program by trial and error, and that explicit LP solves could match the result in about a minute using eight times fewer changed weights.
