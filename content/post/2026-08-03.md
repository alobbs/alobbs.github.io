### SPF Record Syntax: Mechanisms, Qualifiers, Modifiers, and Macros

An SPF record is a single TXT record with strict syntax—a version tag, mechanisms, and modifiers—and any syntax error invalidates the entire record, causing authentication to fail. Eight mechanisms test the client IP, including include, which recursively evaluates another domain’s SPF record and matches only on a Pass result. In practice, most records rely on include, ip4, ip6, and all, and mechanisms listed after "all" are ignored.

### What DMARC Protects You From, and What It Does Not

DMARC does not stop phishing or spam outright; it verifies whether the domain in the visible From line authorized the message through SPF or DKIM alignment. It passes if either SPF or DKIM authenticates and aligns with the visible sender, and fails only when neither does. SPF checks the hidden envelope address, DKIM signs the message, and DMARC ties those results to the sender address users actually see.

- [What DMARC Protects You From, and What It Does Not](https://senderledger.com/articles/what-dmarc-actually-protects-you-from) — *senderledger.com*
