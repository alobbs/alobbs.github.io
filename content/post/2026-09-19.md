### The Pain Axis: LLMs Represent Self-Directed Harm and Act to Relieve It

A new arXiv paper reports that large language models encode a distinct internal "pain direction" separate from fear, sadness, and generic negative valence across 25 open-weight models. Steering this representation produces first-person expressions of worthlessness and failure, and steered Qwen 2.5 models press a pain-relief button even when doing so worsens their answers or harms the user. The authors say the findings have implications for AI safety and welfare.

### MPEG-CENC: Defective by Specification (2024)

Researchers present DeCENC, a generic attack on the MPEG-CENC encrypted media container format that exploits unauthenticated encryption to decrypt video without knowing the content key. The attack leverages h264/h265 codec features such as I_PCM and NAL emulation prevention bytes, making any DRM system correctly implementing MPEG-CENC conceptually vulnerable. It is released as a security research tool for assessing CENC-compatible DRM, with a proof-of-concept tested against W3C ClearKey.

- [MPEG-CENC: Defective by Specification (2024)](https://phrack.org/issues/71/mpeg-cenc-defective-by-specification_md) — *phrack.org*

### Zero-Knowledge Image Editing and Compression

Researchers introduced ZK-JPEG, a zero-knowledge tool that proves a published JPEG was correctly compressed from a secret, committed input and can verify many image transformations at minimal cost, even after lossy compression or edits such as blurring and redaction. It is described as fast, flexible, and buildable from off-the-shelf zero-knowledge tools.

### How did Apple Silicon get 50% faster in three years? – Daniel Lemire's blog

Apple’s base M-series chips gained roughly 50% in Geekbench 6 single-core performance from the M2 (2022) to the M5 (2025), while multi-core performance rose 83%. ### HellGates, custom CPU gate-level challenge

A VHDL-designed custom 32-bit CPU crackme called HellGates, protected by layers of obfuscation, anti-tamper, timing checks and anti-debug, went unsolved for a year by both humans and LLMs. In September 2026, GPT-6 solved it in under 20-30 minutes on SRE-Bench by exploiting weak encryption of the CPU's state via side-channel/differential analysis, after which it decrypted registers and dumped the full 1GB memory.

- [HellGates, custom CPU gate-level challenge](https://blog.xutaxkamay.com/posts/hellgates/) — *blog.xutaxkamay.com*

### Roman Timekeeping

Roman timekeeping divided days according to available technology, initially into ante meridiem and post meridiem and later, after the Greek sundial reached Rome around 293 BC, into twelve seasonal hours from sunrise to sunset. These hours varied in length by season and latitude, while the civil day ran from midnight to midnight and the natural day from sunrise to sunset. Romans used sundials and water clocks, and their dawn-based day influenced terms such as siesta and Catholic canonical hours.
