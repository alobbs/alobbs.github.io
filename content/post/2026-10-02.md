### Fixing GRPO's credit assignment problem without evaluating every step

Researchers introduce ProVer, a framework that improves credit assignment in GRPO for agentic reinforcement learning by using an agentic judge to identify potentially pivotal trajectory segments and then verifying them through terminal success-rate differences between continuations before and after the segment. Verified positive estimates are incorporated into the GRPO advantages for tokens in that segment, avoiding exhaustive evaluation of every intermediate state. ProVer achieves the strongest average performance on ALFWorld, WebShop, and SearchQA, improving over GRPO by 9.91% and 7.12% for Qwen3.5-2B and Qwen3.5-4B, respectively.

- [Fixing GRPO's credit assignment problem without evaluating every step](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36178) — *arxiv.org*

### SequenceHash: multihashing for the rest of us

Trail of Bits introduced SequenceHash and SequenceMAC, open-source hash constructions for secure multihashing with hash functions other than Keccak, including SHA-256/384/512, BLAKE, and RIPEMD. Similar to NIST’s TupleHash but not tied to a single hash function, they aim to prevent attacks caused by ambiguous input encodings and support keys of 32 bytes or longer. ### Thought-Terminating Cliché

A thought-terminating cliché is loaded language, often appearing as folk wisdom, used to shut down argument and bypass cognitive dissonance instead of addressing a point. The term was popularized by Robert Jay Lifton in 1961, who linked such phrases to “the language of non-thought” in totalist environments. Some clichés are not inherently thought-terminating but become so when used to dismiss dissent or justify fallacies.
