### Starlink Signal Leakage Threatens Radio Astronomy's Most Critical Frequencies

A Curtin University study found that unintended electronic leakage from 1,806 Starlink satellites produced 112,534 radio emissions across 73–235 MHz, the frequencies needed by the SKA-Low radio telescope, including ITU-protected bands.
