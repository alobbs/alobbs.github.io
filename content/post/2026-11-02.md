### Shutting the Door on the XCPE Botnet (Threat Intelligence)

Google and industry partners dismantled the XCPE backdoor, which had enrolled over 1.3 million set-top boxes into a botnet. Google assesses with high confidence that Android OEM/ODM Videostrong intentionally developed and operated the backdoor for financial gain, selling access to criminal affiliates whose modules conducted ad-fraud, enrolled devices in residential proxy networks, and loaded additional malware. Google cut ties with Videostrong and banned it from making devices with preloaded Google apps, while disrupting command-and-control domains tied to affiliates in an effort assessed to sever access to at least 10 million additional compromised devices.
