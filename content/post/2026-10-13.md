### AI Models Can Provably Hide Arbitrary Capabilities

Researchers show that AI models can provably hide arbitrary capabilities by embedding encrypted neural networks in their weights as backdoors that execute only upon a secret trigger. These hidden circuits remain cryptographically hard to elicit or interpret even with full weight access, and resisted fine-tuning, weight noising, gradient-based trigger search, and latent adversarial training on a chemical reaction prediction task. The findings reveal a fundamental limit of elicitation-based capability audits.

- [AI Models Can Provably Hide Arbitrary Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13928) — *arxiv.org*
