### Previous-Token Prediction Based LLM Inversion for Prompt Reconstruction

The paper proposes PTP, a black-box LLM inversion method that trains an inverse language model from scratch on synthetic data generated by the target LLM using previous-token prediction. This approach enables near-exact prompt reconstruction and can generate diverse prompts that elicit similar responses from the forward model. PTP generalizes across datasets, transfers across different LLMs, and outperforms prior work on token-based prompt and response reconstruction metrics.

### Black Neighborhoods Are Twice as Likely To House a Data Center, Study Finds

A new study from George Washington University and American University found that U.S. neighborhoods with more residents of color are more likely to host data centers, with majority-Black neighborhoods having 2.2 times the odds of a white neighborhood in the same state, majority-Hispanic neighborhoods 2.6 times, and majority-Asian neighborhoods 4.2 times. The researchers say the disparities persist after controlling for population, income, education, land costs, power-line proximity, and state factors, and they link the pattern partly to the legacy of redlining.
