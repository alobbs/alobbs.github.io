### RTK reports token savings, but our cost benchmarks disagree

A multi-day, $1,500 benchmark of RTK on Terminal-Bench 2.1 found that compressing terminal output did not reliably lower AI coding costs: Fable was 5% cheaper overall but only 3% cheaper per passing task, while DeepSeek was 5% more expensive overall and 7% more expensive per pass, with slightly lower pass rates for both. Task-level analysis showed Fable was 1% more expensive and DeepSeek 17% more expensive with RTK, and almost all of Fable’s apparent savings came from one task. RTK’s reported savings reflect byte reduction rather than billed tokens, so large claims such as 89% do not translate into cheaper runs.

- [RTK reports token savings, but our cost benchmarks disagree](https://quesma.com/blog/does-rtk-make-ai-coding-cheaper/) — *quesma.com*

### Neijuan

Neijuan, or involution, is a Chinese term describing intensified effort, complexity, or competition without corresponding gains in output, efficiency, or collective benefit; it is used to analyze resource inequality, economic malaise, higher education, and labor markets, often leading to overproduction, diminishing returns, and personal overwork, stress, and entrapment.
