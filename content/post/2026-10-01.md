### Engrams, an open source alternative to Devin

Engrams is a self-hosted, open-source orchestrator for AI coding agents that runs each session in its own Firecracker microVM, snapshots idle VMs into deduplicated content-addressed chunks, and restores them quickly on the next prompt. It deploys into a user’s own cloud on GKE or EKS, isolates sessions with KVM and a filtering proxy, and uses OCI images with agent harnesses such as Claude Code and Codex. The 0.x product includes a dashboard, CLI, connectors, and automations, but it is not a hosted service, container runner, developer environment, or model provider.
