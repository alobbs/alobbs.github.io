### Grep beats LSP? Why coding agents ignore your fancier tools

A pilot study found that coding agents often prefer grep over LSP-backed semantic navigation, even when semantic tools are more precise, because LLM-friendliness depends on interface context and usability, not just accuracy. The findings suggest tool effectiveness is shaped by the agent harness and task routing, not just retrieval precision.
