### Grep beats LSP? Why coding agents ignore your fancier tools

A pilot study found that coding agents often prefer grep over LSP-backed semantic navigation, even when semantic tools are more precise, because LLM-friendliness depends on interface context and usability, not just accuracy. Forcing semantic-first tool paths sometimes reduced task success, and semantic navigation only improved precision on noisy codebases without boosting recall or helping on clean ones. The findings suggest tool effectiveness is shaped by the agent harness and task routing, not just retrieval precision.

### UN to vote on adopting new world map that shows Africa's true scale

The UN General Assembly will vote on a resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by African Union members, to phase out the traditional Mercator map in favor of an equal-area projection that more accurately represents Africa’s size. The Mercator map, created for navigation in 1569, distorts equatorial landmasses, making Africa appear roughly the size of Greenland when it is actually 14 times larger. If adopted, the non-binding resolution could influence school curriculums and technology, with supporters arguing it would correct colonial-era misrepresentations of the global south.
