### Software is slow because of bad design, not slow languages

Rewriting software in faster languages like C or Rust generally won’t produce major speedups, since most programs are I/O-bound and slow languages already delegate CPU-heavy work to optimized native libraries. Meaningful performance gains usually come from redesigning software rather than changing languages, with only limited exceptions.
