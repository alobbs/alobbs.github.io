### Harness Engineering 101: The LLM is the brain, the harness is the body

“Harness Engineering 101” is a blog series published as a book that teaches building AI agents from first principles. Its central thesis is that LLM APIs are stateless, so a “harness” must build, maintain, and protect the full conversation array, with “agents” understood as patches to that loop. Across 16 chapters, it develops a ~300-line toy agent in plain Python, with each chapter structured around a failure, its minimal patch, and the patch’s cost.

### Mistral launches time-series foundation models

Mistral has released Timestral, a family of time-series foundation models in four sizes from 5M to 209M parameters that provides probabilistic, multivariate forecasts with past and future covariates. Timestral combines synthetic pre-training, covariate-aware attention, and long-context learning, with fine-tuning available via Mistral Forge.

### Strot – Compile an LLM's intent once, run decisions locally forever

Strot is an open-source decision compiler that uses an LLM once at compile time to turn intent and schema into a frozen Python artifact. At runtime, it performs classification, scoring, routing, and typed decisions locally on CPU at zero marginal cost, returning type-safe JSON with probabilities and confidence while abstaining on low-confidence records. It is installable via pip as `strot-ai` and licensed MIT.

- [Strot – Compile an LLM's intent once, run decisions locally forever](https://github.com/strot-ai/Project-STROT) — *github.com*

### The substitution myth & the tough spot it puts us in

The article argues that AI adoption rests on the “substitution myth”—the false assumption that AI can replace human work one-for-one without changing the broader work system. Instead, AI reshuffles roles and creates new tasks such as configuration, integration, and monitoring, making human cognitive work more critical even as adoption outpaces understanding. As a result, people are often required to intervene when AI fails at exactly the moment they are least prepared to do so.
