### Squircles

A squircle is a shape between a square and a circle with no single mathematical definition; the best-known form is the superellipse, defined by |x|^n + |y|^n = 1, which ranges from a circle at n=2 toward a square as n increases. Squircles drew renewed attention after Apple adopted a custom squircle icon shape in iOS 7 in 2013, later analyzed as a flat-sided squircle rather than a simple superellipse, and Figma has published its own approximation and analysis.
