### Synchronous Queues For VirtIO-FS Show Huge Performance Potential

Sergio López is developing experimental synchronous queues for VirtIO-FS so the host vCPU thread can complete I/O requests before returning to the guest rather than always relying on asynchronous behavior. Early tests show major gains, including cutting libkrunfw build time from 7 minutes 43 seconds to 5 minutes 34 seconds, plus significant random I/O improvements and lower file deletion latency. A pending experimental libkrun pull request would add optional VIRTIO_FS_F_SYNC support.

### Dogs Seem to Agree with Humans That 'Bouba' Is Round and 'Kiki' Is Spiky

A new study found that domestic dogs, like humans, tend to associate the made-up sound “Bouba” with rounded shapes and “Kiki” with spiky shapes. In two experiments with 70 dogs, researchers tracked the animals’ gaze and found they looked more at rounded shapes during “Bouba” and spiky shapes during “Kiki,” even when owners wore headphones to prevent cues. The findings suggest dogs may share a cross-sensory bias linking sounds and shapes, though more research is needed.
