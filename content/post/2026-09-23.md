### Hacker steals 600,000 credit cards while barely lifting a finger

A Chinese-speaking attacker used AI agents to steal more than 600,000 valid credit card records from online retailers and plant skimmers on over 100 websites. US cardholders accounted for 79% of exposed cards, according to Gambit Security.

### Jev Can't Be Calibrated

The article argues that TypeSafe’s Jev, a classifier that returns typed decisions with probabilities, is useful without training data and across tasks, but its advertised calibrated probabilities cannot be guaranteed in general.
