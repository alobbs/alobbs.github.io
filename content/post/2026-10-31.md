## 🔧 Dev & Hardware

### CUDA Oxide 0.3 Vastly Improves Rust Support For NVIDIA CUDA Programming

NVIDIA released CUDA Oxide 0.3, its experimental Rust-to-CUDA compiler, now using stable Rust 1.99.1 instead of nightly builds and unifying its host runtime with cutile-rs. The release adds an experimental Rust-based CUTLASS CuTe implementation, enabling end-to-end Qwen3-8B at BF16 + NVFP4 and Qwen3-TTS on Blackwell GPUs. It also adds full cuda-gdb DWARF debugging, in-kernel event tracing, register/spill diagnostics, improved testing, and performance optimizations for Blackwell and Hopper.

- [CUDA Oxide 0.3 Vastly Improves Rust Support For NVIDIA CUDA Programming](https://www.phoronix.com/news/CUDA-Oxide-0.3) — *phoronix.com*
