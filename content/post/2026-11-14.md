### GLP-1 Drugs May Work Differently in Women

A meta-analysis of randomized trials found that women lost more weight than men while taking GLP-1 receptor agonists, averaging 10.88% versus 6.8% of baseline body weight. Biological differences such as estrogen levels and body composition may help explain the sex difference. Weight loss was otherwise consistent across subgroups by age, race and ethnicity, BMI, and HbA1c.

- [GLP-1 Drugs May Work Differently in Women](https://www.medpagetoday.com/endocrinology/obesity/120124) — *medpagetoday.com*

### Elephants Found to Be Increasingly Isolated Leading to Fractured Social Networks

A 12-year UC San Diego study of more than 440 wild female Asian elephants in Sri Lanka found that population declines have fractured their highly social communities, making groups smaller and more isolated. Researchers warn the population may have crossed a social “tipping point” as deaths, emigration, and fewer new individuals reduce relationships among remaining elephants. This loss of social connections could threaten the animals’ ability to forage, share information, and raise young.

- [Elephants Found to Be Increasingly Isolated Leading to Fractured Social Networks](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/elephants-found-to-be-increasingly-isolated-leading-to-fractured-social-networks) — *today.ucsd.edu*
