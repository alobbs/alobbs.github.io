### Kakistocracy

Kakistocracy is government by the worst, least qualified, or most unscrupulous people, a term coined in the seventeenth century from Greek words meaning "rule by the worst." Historically, it has been used to describe governments such as Robespierre's France, and in modern times it is applied critically to national governments including Russia, the Philippines, Brazil, and the Trump administration in the United States.
