### AI-assisted proof of optimal packing for 11 squares

A complete optimality proof for packing 11 equal squares has passed Lean verification, with all 7,920 local modules accepted and zero admissions reported. The proof establishes an optimal side length of approximately 3.8770835900228141773, defined by a formula involving the unique root of an eighth-degree polynomial. It combines ordinary Lean proofs for geometry and checker soundness with native numerical certificate checks, so the final theorem trusts Lean’s kernel and native compiler.
