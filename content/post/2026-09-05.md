### Trusting-Trust Attack on Linux Distributions

A trusting-trust attack can be implemented via the GNU strip utility rather than a compiler, by manipulating finished ELF binaries. In NixOS, a single tampered strip in the bootstrap seed propagated the payload across generations into the final system, backdooring nearly all binaries, including a complete graphical installer.

- [Trusting-Trust Attack against an Entire Linux Distribution (via strip utility)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24888) — *arxiv.org*

The UN has acknowledged that global warming will surpass the 1.5-degree Celsius Paris accord threshold in the next few years. Instead of abandoning the goal, its new report recommends an “overshoot” strategy: peak temperatures, then bring them back below the limit by ending fossil fuel use and removing carbon dioxide from the air. This path would still bring decades of worsening weather disasters, rising seas, and other climate impacts.

- [Global warming will exceed 1.5-degree limit, UN says](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/global-warming-will-exceed-1-5-degree-limit-un-says-in-report-that-maps-path-back-below-danger-zone) — *pbs.org*

### 'Guilt by association': why 7 int'l aid workers were killed in Israeli airstrike

A Guardian investigation found that seven World Central Kitchen aid workers killed in an Israeli airstrike in April 2024 were targeted under an IDF practice of guilt by association, not simply a mistake. A military prosecutor declined criminal proceedings, despite commanders justifying the strike by claiming the workers had "mixed together" with armed guards, which they equated with Hamas ties. The investigation highlights how this policy endangers civilians, amid over 70,000 Palestinian deaths in the Gaza war.

- ['Guilt by association': why 7 int'l aid workers were killed in Israeli airstrike](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/05/aid-workers-gaza-killed-in-israel-airstrike-investigation) — *theguardian.com*

### NYC PIT Crew

New York City’s Public Interest Technology (PIT) Crew is an in-house team of engineers, designers, and researchers creating digital public goods to improve government interactions for New Yorkers. Their goal is to make these experiences dignified and delightful, and they encourage others to join or subscribe for updates.

### Kai-Fu Lee Q&A: China open-model edge, AI jobs, US chip export curbs, Chinese AI in developing world

Chinese AI companies are narrowing the gap with US rivals despite longstanding restrictions on advanced chips, according to AI pioneer and investor Kai-Fu Lee.
