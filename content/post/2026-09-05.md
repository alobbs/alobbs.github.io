## 🛡️ Security & Cybercrime

### Reversing MikroTik's Silent Patch: The RouterOS 7.23.4 Fix They Wouldn't Explain

MikroTik’s coordinated RouterOS updates (7.23.4, 7.24.2, 6.49.21) silently fixed three bugs, disclosed only by a vague SSH changelog line. Reverse-engineering the NPK binaries exposed two exploitable chains: a low-exponent RSA signature forgery leading to an mtget overflow, and an SSH username of “-2” reaching a legacy login path that lets an authenticated read-only session grant full RouterOS command execution. The researcher explicitly did not reproduce a stock, credential-free method to make SSH accept the literal “-2” username.

- [Reversing MikroTik's Silent Patch: The RouterOS 7.23.4 Fix They Wouldn't Explain](https://npratley.net/reversing-mikrotiks-silent-patch-the-routeros-7-23-4-fix-they-wouldnt-explain/) — *npratley.net*

### Trusting-Trust Attack against an Entire Linux Distribution (via the strip utility)

Researchers demonstrated that Ken Thompson's trusting-trust attack is not specific to compilers by constructing one around the GNU strip utility, which manipulates finished ELF binaries rather than source code. Using a single tampered strip in the NixOS bootstrap seed, the payload propagated across generations and survived into the final system, backdooring nearly all binaries in a complete graphical installer on a real nixpkgs revision.

- [Trusting-Trust Attack against an Entire Linux Distribution (via the strip utility)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24888) — *arxiv.org*

## 🚀 Space & Mobility

### Waymo racked up 8,300 SF parking tickets and nearly $1M in fines

Waymo’s robotaxis have received 8,309 parking tickets in San Francisco since the start of 2025, racking up nearly $1 million in fines—a sharp increase from just 589 tickets in 2024 as the fleet rapidly expanded. The citations, concentrated at busy pickup spots like Fisherman’s Wharf, are viewed as a cost of scale rather than a safety concern, though critics note the fines have done little to alter the company’s behavior.

- [Waymo racked up 8,300 SF parking tickets and nearly $1M in fines](https://electrek.co/2026/09/02/waymo-san-francisco-parking-tickets/) — *electrek.co*

### BepiColombo sheds its ride and starts the final glide to Mercury

After eight years in space, the BepiColombo Mercury probe has separated from its Mercury Transfer Module as it approaches the planet. The uncoupling marks the start of the final phase of its journey to enter orbit around Mercury.
