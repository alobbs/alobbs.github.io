### Excessive time spent online linked to stress and worse mood – study

A study of 900 German adults found that problematic internet usage—characterized by excessive time online—is linked to higher stress, worse mood, and greater neglect of other activities. Temptation was identified as the primary driver of continued use, with pleasure and relief reinforcing the behavior. However, researchers noted that only a small percentage develop pathological patterns, and moderate use can be enriching.
