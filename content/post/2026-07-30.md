## 🤖 AI Policy & Regulation

### GCC steering committee announces AI policy

The GCC steering committee adopted a policy that rejects legally significant contributions (around 15 lines of code/text) containing LLM-generated content, while allowing maintainers to accept LLM-generated test cases. LLM use for research, analysis, and bug discovery is permitted as long as outputs are not included in contributions, and the policy will be periodically revisited.
