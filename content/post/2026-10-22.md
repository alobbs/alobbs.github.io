### When the attacker becomes the user: Rethinking account takeover

Account takeover is a stealthy cyber threat in which attackers obtain credentials through phishing, increasingly aided by generative AI, then quietly monitor compromised email accounts before acting. Instead of immediately launching attacks, they weaponize the account to target employees, systems, customers, and suppliers through outbound phishing that appears to come from a trusted party. This can lead to financial loss, data theft, malware propagation, and reputational damage.

- [When the attacker becomes the user: Rethinking account takeover](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/22/sponsored-when-the-attacker-becomes-the-user-rethinking-account-takeover/5303913) — *theregister.com*

### examining the macOS gcc binary

On macOS, `/usr/bin/gcc` is not a simple alias but a binary that uses `xcselect`/`xcrun` to launch the selected developer tool, rewriting names such as `cc`, `c++`, `llvm-gcc`, and `llvm-g++` to `clang` or `clang++`. Although `xcrun --find gcc` can locate an actual `gcc` in CommandLineTools, that binary also shells out to `xcrun` and ultimately invokes `clang`/`clang++`. Xcode-installed developer tools are generally launched through `xcrun`.
