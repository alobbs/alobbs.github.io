## 🤖 AI & Generative Tech

### Debian Votes To Allow "Responsible Use Of Generative AI"

Debian developers voted to adopt a policy allowing “Responsible Use of Generative AI” in the project. The resolution neither endorses nor prohibits AI-assisted contributions, but requires that all submissions meet existing quality, correctness, maintainability, and legal standards, with contributors responsible for reviewing and modifying AI output. Disclosure of AI assistance is encouraged but not mandatory.

- [Debian Votes To Allow "Responsible Use Of Generative AI"](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Debian-Votes-Responsible-AI-Use) — *phoronix.com*

### Reviving An SD Card With Shorted Capacitors

A faulty 32 GB SD card was repaired by locating a short on its power rail using X-ray and thermal imaging. Two failed miniature capacitors were found and removed, restoring the card and allowing data recovery. The article notes that such passive component failures are common and often repairable enough to recover data.

### Samsung's Processing-in-Memory (PIM)

Samsung’s LPDDR5X-PIM places processing-in-memory blocks with MAC arrays in each of its 16 DRAM banks, unlocking 614 GB/s of internal bandwidth and 2.4 TOPS for low-precision workloads. It remains compatible with standard LPDDR5X memory controllers by using special row addresses to access PIM registers and switch between single-bank and multi-bank modes.

- [Samsung's Processing-in-Memory (PIM)](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/hot-chips-2026-samsungs-processing) — *chipsandcheese.com*

### TurboKV: Insanely fast Rust key-value store

TurboKV is an async embedded key-value store for Rust that supports atomic batches, ordered range scans, configurable durability presets, compression, and background compaction. It requires Tokio and hardware AES support, and offers APIs for database operations including CRUD, batch writes, and range queries.

- [TurboKV: Insanely fast Rust key-value store](https://github.com/kingroryg/turbokv) — *github.com*
