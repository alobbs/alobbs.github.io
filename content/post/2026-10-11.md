### Don't Put Tilde In Your Path

The article warns that using `export PATH="$PATH:~/.local/bin/"` does not expand `~` to your home directory, because tilde expansion only occurs in unquoted contexts. Instead, it adds a literal relative path such as `./~/.local/bin/`, which can cause binaries to be executed from an unexpected directory. Users should replace `~` with `$HOME` in their shell configs and check their `PATH` for any `~` entries.
