### How a device finds encrypted DNS by itself

DDR lets a device ask its current DNS resolver for `_dns.resolver.arpa` to discover encrypted endpoints (DoH, DoT, DoQ) with hostname, port, and preference, then automatically upgrades to the matching protocol. The reply can be tailored to the resolver profile and includes endpoint IPs to avoid an extra plaintext lookup. But since the discovery starts over plain DNS, the exchange can be tampered with, making the upgrade opportunistic rather than guaranteed secure.
