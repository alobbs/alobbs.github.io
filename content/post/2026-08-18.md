### Linux 7.3 improves performance when running out of vRAM

Running out of VRAM is primarily a performance issue, not a stability one, because the GPU must fetch evicted data from CPU RAM over the slower PCIe bus, which limits bandwidth and framerate. Linux 7.3 includes kernel patches to improve this VRAM overcommit handling.

### Voluntary attention regulates acute immune responses in humans

Voluntary attention directed toward bodily sensations during acute skin inflammation produced more regulated immune responses than distraction, with roughly 1.5-fold smaller inflammatory responses in about 90% of participants across two cohorts. The effect involved both sensory modulation of identical input and top-down parasympathetic vagal activity, establishing attention as a cognitive mechanism capable of regulating immune function in vivo.

### Tsampi BFT: Leaderless One-Round Voting with Parameterized Finality

Tsampi BFT is a leaderless Byzantine fault-tolerant protocol that achieves exact-lineage finality with one vote round per block, using a dual-linked vote chain to preserve endorsement order and require strictly better successors. Finality requires two strict-supermajority vote passes, Q1 and Q2, where Q2 is independently deduplicated and only counts later votes from descendant blocks. Proposal admission can be parameterized separately, but with an unchanged validator set and two-thirds endorsement, genesis reaches finality by Block 3 with a steady one-block lag.
