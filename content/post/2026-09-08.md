### Why getting your hands dirty is good for you

Contact with soil and natural materials boosts the skin's microbial diversity, helping defend against harmful bacteria and supporting immune and skin health. Research in Finland found that even brief exposure to soil, peat, or moss increased skin microbes, while children who played regularly in forest settings had greater microbial diversity and fewer pathogens. These benefits require frequent or prolonged contact, as the effects can quickly fade.
