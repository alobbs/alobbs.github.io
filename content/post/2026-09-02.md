## 🤖 AI & Machine Learning

### LLM Judges Verify Presence, Not Absence: Omission Blindness in AI Clinical Notes

LLM judges evaluating AI-generated clinical notes reliably detect added or altered content but largely fail to flag omitted information, performing near chance on omissions. Restructuring the evaluation—listing transcript facts and checking each against the note—markedly improves omission detection, though omissions whose facts appear elsewhere still evade detection. The authors release their benchmark, prompts, and judgments.

- [LLM Judges Verify Presence, Not Absence: Omission Blindness in AI Clinical Notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31016) — *arxiv.org*

### The Emergent Symbolic Structure of Artificial Neural Networks

The paper proposes that neural networks implicitly encode symbolic structure, despite using continuous vector representations. The authors show that vector representations across various networks, including LLMs in arithmetic, logic, code, and language, can be closely approximated by closed-form symbolic equations. This approximation enables targeted interventions on LLM behavior, indicating the networks rely on the identified symbolic structures.

- [The Emergent Symbolic Structure of Artificial Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29530) — *arxiv.org*

### Bye Bye Perspective API: Lessons for Measurement Infrastructure in NLP, CSS and LLM Evaluation

The impending closure of the Perspective API exposes how heavily NLP research has relied on external toxicity tools they did not control, resulting in overclaimed findings, unstable results from silent model updates, and unexplained disparities across 241 surveyed papers. The authors argue the field must build and govern its own measurement infrastructure, releasing 5.9 million Perspective scores from 77 datasets and proposing ten requirements to guide such efforts.

- [Bye Bye Perspective API: Lessons for Measurement Infrastructure in NLP, CSS and LLM Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25580) — *arxiv.org*

### Read your own writes, off the primary

PostgreSQL 19 introduces a `WAIT FOR LSN` command that lets standby databases block until a specified WAL position is replayed, ensuring reads reflect recent writes. This directly addresses replica lag, which causes newly saved changes to briefly disappear in fast frontend apps, and offers an alternative to workarounds like primary pinning or Redis flags. The article also examines what causes replay lag and measures the command's effectiveness against older strategies.

## ⚡ Energy & Telecom

### Why are there no flow batteries with symmetric ferrocyanide electrolytes?

Ferrocyanide/ferricyanide is common in asymmetric flow batteries due to its stability and kinetics, but it cannot be used in symmetric systems because it decomposes under strong reducing potentials, releasing free cyanide and iron that form precipitates. It also forms insoluble Prussian blue with heavy metal cations, and even chelating strategies fail because the decomposition is irreversible and damaging. This instability outside its narrow redox range makes symmetric ferrocyanide flow batteries economically unviable and unsafe.

- [Why are there no flow batteries with symmetric ferrocyanide electrolytes?](https://chemisting.com/2026/09/02/why-are-there-no-flow-batteries-with-symmetric-ferrocyanide-electrolytes/) — *chemisting.com*
