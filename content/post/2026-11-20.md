### Crafting Targeted MP4 Fuzzers

The post explains why conventional mutation-based fuzzing struggles with the MP4 container format, particularly because insertions or deletions require correcting atom size fields and ancestor sizes to preserve structure. ### Windows 11 lock screen bypassed via Narrator, no patch available

A researcher has demonstrated a Windows 11 lock screen bypass, dubbed FastUnlock, that uses Narrator and the “Forgot my PIN” screen to run code and access files on locked BitLocker-encrypted PCs. ThreatLocker reproduced the exploit, which Microsoft has not patched; a BitLocker preboot PIN may help when affected machines are off or hibernated.

### UK fridges secured from hackers in secret nationwide operation

### OpenSSL: Buffer overflow in EVP_EncodeBlock() (CVE-2026-121481, CVSS 9.8)

A proposed OpenSSL patch fixes a buffer overflow in EVP_EncodeBlock() (CVE-2026-121481, CVSS 9.8) by returning -1 for invalid inputs and adds a similar guard for EVP_DecodeBlock(). ### Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI

AI systems autonomously designed and verified Frostbite, a new general-purpose CPU architecture with a new instruction set built on interaction nets, iterating across compiler, ISA, microarchitecture, RTL, verification, and final GDS. Across 25 workloads, including 15 held out until final evaluation, Frostbite achieved a 4.59x performance-per-watt improvement over a modern silicon-proven superscalar CPU core. The work demonstrates AI-driven hardware co-design can discover novel architectural primitives and deliver step-function gains in performance and area efficiency.

- [Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite) — *architectlabs.com*

### Mapping every neuron in Qwen3-8B

Researchers mapped individual neurons in Qwen3-8B and other models, finding that nudging a single neuron can reliably trigger specific behaviors such as speaking like a pirate, switching to German, or adopting a persona. The work used dose-response tests and random-neuron controls to distinguish meaningful neurons from arbitrary ones among hundreds of thousands. It suggests some model behaviors are tied to sparse, interpretable internal controls.

- [Mapping every neuron in Qwen3-8B](https://www.fermionresearch.com/research/mapping-every-neuron-in-qwen3-8b/) — *fermionresearch.com*
