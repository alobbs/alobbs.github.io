### Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI

AI systems autonomously designed and verified Frostbite, a new general-purpose CPU architecture with a new instruction set built on interaction nets, iterating across compiler, ISA, microarchitecture, RTL, verification, and final GDS. Across 25 workloads, including 15 held out until final evaluation, Frostbite achieved a 4.59x performance-per-watt improvement over a modern silicon-proven superscalar CPU core. The work demonstrates AI-driven hardware co-design can discover novel architectural primitives and deliver step-function gains in performance and area efficiency.

- [Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite) — *architectlabs.com*

### Mapping every neuron in Qwen3-8B
