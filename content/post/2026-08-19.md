### Solo: a .so loader for static Linux binaries

### a filename when none exists

### Scientists stunned by children's lung recovery in ultra low emission zone

A London study found that children’s lung growth, previously stunted by air pollution, rapidly recovered after the 2019 Ultra Low Emission Zone reduced emissions. Over four years, lung capacity in London children caught up to levels seen in a less-polluted comparison group in Luton, with clinically impaired capacity falling from 14% to 9%. Researchers called the improvement "stunning" and the strongest evidence yet that clean air zones can reverse childhood pollution harm.

### Amazon appears to have leaked Jason Statham's 'Mutiny' in full on Prime Video US two days early

Jason Statham's new film *Mutiny* was accidentally leaked on Amazon's Prime Video in the US, where the full 1-hour-35-minute movie is now available to stream two days before its scheduled theatrical release. Neither the production studios nor the US distributor Lionsgate are owned by Amazon, suggesting the release was unintentional, and Amazon has been contacted for comment.
