### OpenSSH SSHole flaw actively exploited

CISA has confirmed active exploitation of the OpenSSH/musl flaw dubbed “SSHole,” which lets attackers log in to vulnerable systems—including passwordless root accounts—using any password longer than 256 characters. The decade-old bug affects OpenSSH on musl-based Linux distributions such as Alpine, OpenWrt, Buildroot, and Yocto when PAM is disabled. CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, requiring federal agencies to apply fixes by November 12.
