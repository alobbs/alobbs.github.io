### x86 Ecosystem Advisory Group Posts ACE v2 & AVX10.3 Specs, GCC Patches Ready

The x86 Ecosystem Advisory Group led by AMD and Intel has posted ACE v2 and AVX10.3 architecture specifications, though no processors with ACE or AVX10.2 have shipped yet. ACE v2 adds reduced-precision 4-bit and expanded 8-bit formats plus FP16/FP32/FP64 support, while AVX10.3 brings new vectorization and conversion instructions. GCC patches for the new ACE and AVX10.3 instructions are already ready for review ahead of likely CPU availability in 2028–2029.
