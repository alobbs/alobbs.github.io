### Forging 1024-bit RSA signatures in nearly SNFS time

Researchers implemented a 2007 Joux–Naccache–Thomé algorithm that forges RSA signatures after temporary access to a raw signing/decryption oracle, without factoring the key, in time close to SNFS. For 1024-bit RSA, the attack took 1,380 CPU core-years over five months and 232 oracle queries, with subsequent offline forgery of any signature taking 180 core-years; it was demonstrated against an HSM via black-box API and applies to blind RSA. Extrapolation suggests RSA with a signing oracle has 15–30 bits less security than factoring-based estimates for 1024- to 4096-bit keys, and even 4096-bit RSA may not meet 128-bit security in this model.

- [Forging 1024-bit RSA signatures in nearly SNFS time](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2131.pdf) — *eprint.iacr.org*
