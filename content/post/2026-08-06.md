### Zapscape (CVE-2026-64561)

Zapscape (CVE-2026-64561) is a KVM/x86 guest-to-host escape vulnerability caused by a use-after-free in the shadow MMU recursive zap path, allowing a guest to execute code on the host with kernel root privileges. The PoC targets AMD via QEMU TCG and demonstrates the full exploit chain, though it is not a weaponized cloud exploit and requires porting to real environments. ### LLMs won't break symmetric crypto

Anthropic's LLM Claude Mythos found cryptanalytic attacks, but they are not practical: a key-recovery attack on HAWK-512 reduces its security from 128-bit to at most 108 bits, and an attack on 7-round AES poses no threat to full AES. The article argues LLMs won't break established symmetric crypto due to the robust, messy structure of these designs, though it sees value in using LLMs to formalize cryptanalytic techniques and find errors in proofs.

Zak van der Merwe's career at AWS building these systems for EC2 and DSQL shows they present hard distributed systems problems where key decisions determine whether a service survives growth. The article frames control planes as unsung heroes that keep cloud infrastructure running reliably without manual intervention for every failure.
