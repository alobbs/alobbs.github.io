### Google launches Gemini 3.8 Live models

### 1Password AI patching benchmark misleading

1Password’s AI patching benchmark is misleading because its headline 26% clean-fix rate included deliberately wrong fixes, trials where agents could not compile or test patches, a hard vulnerability sample, and inconsistent model settings. A reanalysis of the study’s data found that 86% of patches—2,634 of 3,067—blocked the supplied exploit under fairer conditions when agents could run code and were not given bad instructions. The report warns that the misleading headline could discourage defenders from using AI patching and leave repairable vulnerabilities unaddressed.
