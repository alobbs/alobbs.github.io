### AI Has a Discovery Problem

AI adoption is hindered by a “discovery problem”: users cannot see what AI can do because its capabilities are hidden behind a blank prompt, and they don’t know what to ask for. Templates and contextual suggestions offer only partial fixes, as they often fail to match an individual’s specific work. Ultimately, the system itself must reveal its potential capabilities gradually and contextually, rather than placing the burden of discovery on the user.

- [AI Has a Discovery Problem](https://mhacevedo.com/posts/the-discovery-problem) — *mhacevedo.com*

### The Nonfiction Book Market is Collapsing?

Over the past decade, fiction and nonfiction have essentially flipped among top hardcover bestsellers: in 2015, only 6 of the top 25 were fiction, but in 2025, 18 were fiction and just 7 were nonfiction. Nonfiction, once seen as the more stable and predictable segment due to expert and celebrity authors, has suffered a collapse in demand. This decline stems less from media fragmentation and more from a fundamental loss of reader interest, pushing publishers toward fiction’s higher potential returns.
