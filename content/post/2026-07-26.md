### Maybe we should revisit microkernels

Microkernels provide better security, reliability, and modularity by keeping only scheduling, I/O access management, and IPC in the kernel, but historically suffered from high overhead due to context switches. Modern hardware features like IOMMUs and shared memory with atomic operations eliminate context switches in the happy path, making microkernels viable again.
