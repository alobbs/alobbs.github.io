### An ESP32 based plane radar for my desk

A project turns an ESP32-C3 and round display into a live aircraft radar using ADS-B data, showing flight details, origin/destination, and weather. The author customized the firmware with authenticated OTA updates, but notes the original 3D enclosure has overly tight tolerances and plans to design a more forgiving replacement.

## 🤖 AI & Robotics

### Elio raises $21M Series A led by Innovation Endeavors and Xora for AI image sensor

Elio, founded by former Meta AR/VR executives Nadav Grossinger and Nitay Romano, raised $21 million in Series A funding to commercialize a new type of image sensor designed for artificial intelligence rather than human vision. The sensor uses dynamic optical layers with micromirrors to extract only relevant information in real time, allowing AI to determine what to capture instead of processing a fixed image. Innovation Endeavors and Xora led the round, with participation from Kevin Weil, Scribble VC, UpWest, and Resolute Ventures.

- [Elio raises $21M Series A led by Innovation Endeavors and Xora for AI image sensor](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkhattyhzl) — *calcalistech.com*

### Inflect-Micro-v2: complete voice in 9.36M parameters

Inflect-Micro-v2 is a compact local text-to-waveform TTS model with under 10 million parameters, offering fixed-voice English synthesis, deterministic seeds, and long-text handling on CPU or CUDA. In evaluations, it achieved a 66.2% human preference rate, a UTMOS22 score of 4.395, and a two-ASR semantic word error rate of 3.99%.

- [Inflect-Micro-v2: complete voice in 9.36M parameters](https://huggingface.co/owensong/Inflect-Micro-v2) — *huggingface.co*

Ruff v0.16.0 significantly expands its default rule set from 59 to 413 rules, adding catches for syntax and runtime errors from flake8-bugbear and pyupgrade. ### OpenSlides – Open-source desktop app for animated code presentations

OpenSlides is a free, open-source desktop app that converts code into presentation slides with step-by-step transitions and highlights, operating entirely offline and keeping projects local and private. It offers features like Magic Move effects, customizable themes, and keyboard navigation, and is available for macOS, Windows, and Linux as a subscription-free alternative to other code presentation tools.

- [OpenSlides – Open-source desktop app for animated code presentations](https://github.com/codewiththiha/OpenSlides) — *github.com*

### W4ME Station – a WASM-4 runtime for Java ME phones

W4ME Station is a runtime that allows unmodified WASM-4 WebAssembly cartridges to run on Java ME phones (CLDC 1.1/MIDP 2.0). It ships with thirteen bundled games in a sub-300 KB JAR, supports multiple input methods and persistent storage, and requires no JIT or network connection. The project offers two download variants, one with optional JSR-75 file browsing.

- [W4ME Station – a WASM-4 runtime for Java ME phones](https://github.com/mulfyx/w4me-station) — *github.com*

## 🔬 Science & Nature

### Humans haven't stopped evolving

A new study using ancient DNA and advanced statistical methods reveals that human evolution has accelerated over the past 10,000 years, with natural selection intensifying in the last 5,000. Researchers identified 479 genes under selection, many linked to immunity, contradicting previous assumptions that human evolution had slowed or stopped.

- [Humans haven't stopped evolving](https://www.harvardmagazine.com/research/harvard-human-evolution-genes-selective-pressure) — *harvardmagazine.com*

### Alien World Chemistry Found Inside Meteorite That Struck New Jersey Home

A meteorite that crashed through a New Jersey home in 2024 was identified as a rare CM1/2 carbonaceous chondrite, containing preserved fragments from a primitive asteroid that had been altered by ancient briny fluids. The quick recovery of the pristine fragments allowed scientists to study evidence of concentrated salty fluids from near the asteroid's surface, a process not previously observed in this meteorite type.

- [Alien World Chemistry Found Inside Meteorite That Struck New Jersey Home](https://www.seti.org/news/alien-world-chemistry-found-inside-meteorite/) — *seti.org*

### Possible invasive species superspreader event near Strait of Hormuz

Over 1,500 ships stalled near the Strait of Hormuz due to ongoing conflict risk triggering a superspreader event of invasive species, as prolonged idle periods allow hardy marine organisms to accumulate on hulls and be transported globally. These biofouling organisms, adapted to extreme conditions, pose a high risk of surviving long voyages and causing significant ecological and economic damage in foreign ecosystems.

- [Possible invasive species superspreader event near Strait of Hormuz](https://abcnews.com/International/scientists-warn-invasive-species-superspreader-event-due-stalling/story?id=135051186) — *abcnews.com*

### What 180k words look like as a temporal knowledge graph (Oz series)
