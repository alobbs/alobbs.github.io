### Recurrent Looped Transformer

Recurrent Looped Transformer (RLT) combines a causal encoder with a recurrent decoder that carries its hidden state and layerwise sliding-window attention KV cache across all prompt and response tokens, so after \(t\) tokens the recurrent path traverses \(tL_D\) decoder blocks while per-token compute stays fixed. The article outlines RLT’s pretraining, SFT, generation, and RL replay procedures, emphasizing batched encoder work, token-ordered decoder updates, full BPTT, and cache rebuilding for exact current-policy replay. It notes that realized reasoning gains, hardware efficiency, and RL scaling remain to be established.

- [Recurrent Looped Transformer](https://yifanzhang-pro.github.io/recurrent-looped-tranformer/) — *yifanzhang-pro.github.io*

### Docket – Per-commit evidence records for agent-written code

### Origin of x86 ud2 instruction name

The x86 `ud2` instruction is an architecturally undefined instruction that reliably raises an invalid-opcode exception and is often emitted by compilers to mark unreachable code or force a crash if reached. It is named `ud2` because the older unofficial invalid-opcode byte sequences `0F FF` and `0F B9` were retroactively named `ud0` and `ud1`, leaving Intel’s officially recommended undefined instruction as `ud2`.

### Global shortage has led to motor oil rationing at Costco

Costco’s Kirkland Signature full-synthetic motor oil has jumped to $57.99 for a 10-quart case, up from the mid-$30 range, and is now limited to two units per member every seven days; Mobil 1 purchases are also restricted. The changes reflect tightening lubricant supply and higher costs to produce oils meeting stricter modern engine specifications, reducing the usual savings from DIY oil changes and signaling broader pressure in the motor oil market.

### Analyst Index – analysts who make money telling you good stock calls

Analyst Index is a new platform where analysts publish price targets with maturity dates and are scored against actual stock performance, aiming to build records based on accuracy rather than follower count. It plans a subscription model where analysts set their own prices and keep 90%, contrasting with TipRanks and Benzinga, which scrape sell-side calls without sharing revenue. The site currently has 44 price targets from 25 analysts across 3 tickers but lacks analyst signup, payments, and paywall enforcement, and it faces open questions about cold-start supply and whether price targets carry real information.

- [Analyst Index – analysts who make money telling you good stock calls](https://www.analystidx.com/) — *analystidx.com*

### Nvidia dismisses "circular financing", says every $1 it invests brings back $100

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang dismissed concerns about circular financing, saying rhetorically that every $1 Nvidia invests brings back $100. The company has invested about $50 billion in AI labs and provided up to $105 billion in guarantees for OpenAI’s Ohio data-center project, where it is the exclusive chip supplier, while a platform aims to mobilize $500 billion in third-party capital. Nvidia shares fell 2.37% for a third straight session amid broader market weakness, with investors still questioning whether AI infrastructure demand is truly independent.

- [Nvidia dismisses "circular financing", says every $1 it invests brings back $100](https://invezz.com/news/2026/09/11/nvidia-says-every-1-it-invests-brings-back-100-so-why-does-the-stock-keep-falling/) — *invezz.com*
