### AI for scientific discovery: How researchers use Claude

A report analyzing 207,000 Claude and Claude Code research sessions finds scientists use AI mainly for data analysis and writing, especially in formal sciences like computer science and mathematics, and less for idea generation and data gathering despite ranking those tasks as important for discovery. Claude use tracks AI capability, with tasks AI performs well receiving disproportionately more use.
