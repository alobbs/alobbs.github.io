An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone

The paper proposes a background-independent algebraic quantization of vacuum general relativity on four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetimes, solving the gravitational constraints on intersecting null cones and promoting the reduced phase space to commutation relations at finite (G_N). It constructs a nonperturbative Hilbert space and Lorentzian path integral with absolutely convergent sums over intermediate states, computes graviton pair creation nonperturbatively, and shows that macroscopic causal diamonds have finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. The framework yields the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy and its logarithmic correction for large Schwarzschild black holes, recovering Sen’s one-loop coefficient, and derives the semiclassical Einstein equations to one loop as its low-energy limit.

Unconditional Integer Factorization in Subexponential Time

The paper presents an unconditional, zero-error Las Vegas algorithm for integer factorization in subexponential time on a single fixed uniform classical multitape Turing machine. Every prime factor is deterministically certified via Agrawal–Kayal–Saxena primality testing and direct multiplication, and a Lean 4 formalization of the proof has been completed for release in a companion submission. The paper and formalization were produced entirely by Cogentic, an agentic framework using Gemini 4, with no human interaction beyond the initial prompt.

Lions’ Commentary on Unix: Why It Was Secretly Copied for 20 Years

John Lions prepared lecture notes in May 1976 explaining AT&T’s Unix Version 6 kernel source, and the commentary circulated among Unix-licensed schools and research institutions. After AT&T’s 1979 license changes restricted discussing source code, distribution was tightly controlled and students used unauthorized photocopies for about 20 years. The official edition was finally published in 1996 after SCO granted permission.

A perfectly normal scripting language

NormalScript is a pure-JavaScript, MIT-licensed bytecode VM for a modern strict subset of JavaScript that embeds in Node ≥24 or Bun ≥1.4 apps without WASM, native addons, workers, or isolates. Minipg: PostgreSQL as a Single Executable

minipg packages PostgreSQL as a single executable with no server to configure: a .pgdb directory holds the data, config, credentials, and Postgres version, while commands open a psql shell or auto-start an idle-stopping server as needed. It embeds Docker Hub’s postgres:18-alpine image and runs it with h9, a Zig library whose Linux personality executes Alpine Postgres binaries directly on the host hypervisor without a Linux kernel, VM image, or Docker. The MIT-licensed project builds with Zig 0.16 and includes a browser demo that requires JavaScript Promise Integration.
