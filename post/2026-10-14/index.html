AIR-LLM: Broadcasting AI Weights over Radio for Memory-Free Edge LLM Inference via RF Computing

Researchers present AIR-LLM, an architecture that enables memory-free edge LLM inference by broadcasting model weights from a central radio and computing GEMV directly in the RF domain using RF mixers. It uses MIMO spatial multiplexing and a precoder–postcoder pair to reduce airtime and allow one broadcast to serve unlimited users. Evaluated on ray-traced urban channels and real RF mixer profiles, AIR-LLM achieved 4.0% WikiText-2 perplexity degradation on LLaMA-3.1-8B while saving 157.7/40.4 energy and shortening airtime 104.1/26.0 versus FP16 and weight-only quantization baselines.
