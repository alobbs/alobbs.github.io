OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra independently broke the long-resistant German Army Enigma message MVUEH (Nr. 172) from 10 July 1941, developing its own Enigma simulator and Bombe software and using a repeated “ROSENOW” crib to recover a key with wheel order 253. Drop – a rootless Linux sandbox with gVisor support

Drop is a rootless, language-independent Linux sandbox that enforces isolation for third-party programs, inspired by Python’s virtualenv.
