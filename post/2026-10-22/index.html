When the attacker becomes the user: Rethinking account takeover

Account takeover is a stealthy cyber threat in which attackers obtain credentials through phishing, increasingly aided by generative AI, then quietly monitor compromised email accounts before acting. Instead of immediately launching attacks, they weaponize the account to target employees, systems, customers, and suppliers through outbound phishing that appears to come from a trusted party. This can lead to financial loss, data theft, malware propagation, and reputational damage.
