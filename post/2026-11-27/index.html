🔐 Security & Privacy

Hackers strike AFP taking global news platform offline

AFP took its AFP Forum content platform offline after detecting an overnight cyberattack, while its core news production systems and multilingual newsrooms continued operating normally. The agency found evidence that attackers used client FTP credentials and urged affected clients to reset their logins. AFP has notified French authorities and is providing alternative access to subscribers while the platform remains unavailable.

Apple flagged Wukong, a Chinese food delivery app, for containing SparkCat malware that scans users’ image galleries with OCR to find crypto recovery phrases and private keys. A newer App Store version was released under a different publisher but did not state that the malware was removed. Users are advised not to install the app.

UK benefits services offline after cyber incident

The UK Department for Work and Pensions took its benefits systems offline after a cyber incident, blocking new Universal Credit and Personal Independence Payment claims while regular benefit and pension payments continue normally. Officials said the incident was not ransomware and did not involve money being moved, and the DWP is working with cybersecurity agencies as experts warned scammers may exploit the disruption with phishing messages. The department holds about 27 petabytes of data, including personal information such as bank details, and it is not yet known whether that data was affected.

The Cutthroat Business of Shipwrecks

In 1627, two Portuguese “floating cities” shipwrecked off Bordeaux, spilling luxury goods onto French beaches and sparking a dispute between cargo owners and coastal residents who claimed the goods under the ancient “right of wreck.” The custom gave local lords or finders claims to wrecked property, but centuries of legal wrangling reshaped it as international trade grew. Courts developed distinctions such as flotsam and jetsam, and owners were generally entitled to recover their goods in exchange for a fee or salvage reward.
