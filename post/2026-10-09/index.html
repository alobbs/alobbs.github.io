Contributors describe the results as stunning and field-shaking, citing advances such as Thompson’s group F being non-amenable and the Boone–Higman conjecture being solved, while warning that mathematics research will need a massive transformation.

What should we tell our students?

In a guest post, Álvaro Lozano-Robledo addresses undergraduate and graduate math students anxious about their academic futures amid advances in large language models. Inspired by a distressed student’s questions about whether math academia will remain accessible and whether AI will surpass humans in pure math research within 5–10 years, he warns that the field risks losing an entire generation of mathematicians. His TL;DR advice is to keep calm and carry on studying math.

“Robot” Is A Social Construct: Why Your Dishwasher Is Not A Robot

The article argues that “robot” is a social construct rather than a strict technical definition, rooted in science fiction and cultural perceptions. It contends that while a dishwasher is an automated machine, it is not a robot because the term is applied to technologies that feel new, unsettling, or humanlike, not merely to automated systems.
