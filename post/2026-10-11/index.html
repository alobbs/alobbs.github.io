What Is Umami?

Umami is a savory taste first characterized in Japan in the early 20th century and accepted as the fifth basic taste in the early 2000s after its taste receptors were identified. Although common in Asian cooking, umami remained relatively unfamiliar and hard to describe in the West because pure umami compounds are rare.
