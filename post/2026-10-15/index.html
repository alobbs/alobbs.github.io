Speculative Decoding: The Art of Guessing Well

Speculative decoding reduces LLM inference latency by using a smaller model to guess several tokens ahead while the larger target model verifies them in parallel, without changing the final sampling distribution. The article derives the method from first principles and surveys modern drafters such as EAGLE-3, multi-token prediction, and block-diffusion systems like DFlash and DSpark. It also discusses how to train drafters and why minimizing KL divergence is not quite the desired objective.

ArenaAllocators don’t play nicely with ArrayLists

ArrayLists interact poorly with ArenaAllocators because growth allocates a new buffer, copies data, then frees the old buffer, meaning the freed memory is not the last allocation and cannot be reclaimed by the arena.
