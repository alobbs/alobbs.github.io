GPU-unlock: FP/BF16 instruction issue rate on Nvidia 3000’s cards

GPU-unlock 1.0.0 is a Linux kernel module that unlocks FP/BF16 instruction issue rates on Nvidia 3000-series GA102/GA103/GA104/GA106 cards to professional-equivalent levels, doubling FP/BF16 FMA-to-FP32 issue speed. It combines the CMP unlocker’s V67/PLM technique with pre-driver timing, writes SS0/SS1 speed selectors, and performs FLR before the unmodified NVIDIA driver binds, avoiding per-driver patching. On a 3090 Ti, reported dense 8192³ GEMM medians rose from 87.46 to 118.39 TFLOPS FP16, 87.50 to 157.41 TFLOPS BF16, and 42.58 to 82.61 TFLOPS TF32.
