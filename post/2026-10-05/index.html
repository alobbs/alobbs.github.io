Dust: Pretraining Transformers Without Backpropagation

Dust is a new zeroth-order optimization method that pretrains transformer language models without backpropagation by perturbing activations independently at every token, allowing one forward pass to evaluate many virtual population members in parallel. It approximates or sometimes exceeds backprop at large population sizes and is orders of magnitude more efficient than weight-space evolution strategies. The authors report that larger models are more population-efficient and that gradient estimates remain aligned with backprop up to 1B tokens.

  • Dust: Pretraining Transformers Without Backpropagation — qlabs.sh
