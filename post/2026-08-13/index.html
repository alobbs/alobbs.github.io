Spaghettifying DRAM

A new tool called skitter-creek-bath-salts rewrites physical DRAM address translations to scramble platform memory and expose hidden protected regions, unlocking features such as the PSP, SMM, C6 DRAM, and CPU microcode. It targets AMD Family 16h CPUs, where the DRAM controller’s translation registers are documented but cannot be locked. The write-up details how breaking these address translations undermines the security primitives built on top of the memory hierarchy.

Tocharian Online

Tocharian A and B are two closely related Indo-European languages attested in documents from Silk Road sites in Chinese Turkestan, dating to roughly the sixth–eighth centuries AD. Most surviving texts are Buddhist translations, with Tocharian A found only in the Turfan/Qārāšahr area and Tocharian B throughout the region. Because secular documents survive only in Tocharian B, scholars suggest Tocharian A may already have been an extinct liturgical language.
