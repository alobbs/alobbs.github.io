LLMs won’t break symmetric crypto

Anthropic’s LLM Claude Mythos found cryptanalytic attacks, but they are not practical: a key-recovery attack on HAWK-512 reduces its security from 128-bit to at most 108 bits, and an attack on 7-round AES poses no threat to full AES. The article argues LLMs won’t break established symmetric crypto due to the robust, messy structure of these designs, though it sees value in using LLMs to formalize cryptanalytic techniques and find errors in proofs.
