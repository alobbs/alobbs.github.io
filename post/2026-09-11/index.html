Cherenkov Radiation - traveling faster than light

Cherenkov radiation is the blue or violet glow produced when charged particles travel faster than light in a medium, a discovery by Pavel Cherenkov recognized with the 1958 Nobel Prize in Physics. Neijuan

Neijuan, or involution, is a Chinese term describing intensified effort, complexity, or competition without corresponding gains in output, efficiency, or collective benefit; it is used to analyze resource inequality, economic malaise, higher education, and labor markets, often leading to overproduction, diminishing returns, and personal overwork, stress, and entrapment. The concept originated in anthropology through Alexander Goldenweiser and Clifford Geertz and later entered Chinese rural studies via Prasenjit Duara and Philip Huang.

Palantir CEO says US general asked him to build an “alien anti-gravity machine”

Palantir CEO Alex Karp claimed an unnamed US general asked the company to build an “alien anti-gravity machine,” but gave no branch, program, or evidence that it was an official request. He used the anecdote to argue that Palantir engineers should challenge unrealistic customer demands. The remark drew skeptical reactions on Reddit’s UFO forum.

Did someone actually turn a fly bisexual?

Nico Christie claimed he made a digital male fruit fly “bisexual” by blocking mAL output neurons in a 166,606-neuron MaleCNS model. Although the simulation responded more to male cues, female cues still produced stronger responses, so it did not show a clear male preference. The experiment reflects growing interest in digital fly-brain simulations, which scientists study because fly brains are simpler than human brains while sharing many genes.
