AI Has a Discovery Problem

AI adoption is hindered by a “discovery problem”: users cannot see what AI can do because its capabilities are hidden behind a blank prompt, and they don’t know what to ask for. Templates and contextual suggestions offer only partial fixes, as they often fail to match an individual’s specific work. Ultimately, the system itself must reveal its potential capabilities gradually and contextually, rather than placing the burden of discovery on the user.

Solaris Turnstiles

Solaris introduced turnstiles to manage blocking mutexes, addressing two key problems: keeping mutexes small by externalizing wait queues and preventing priority inversion through priority inheritance.
