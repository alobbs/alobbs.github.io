Parametron: 50s Japanese computer that uses neither transistors nor vacuum tubes

The parametron, a logic element using ferrite cores and parametric oscillation, was invented in 1954 by Eiichi Goto at the University of Tokyo. It powered the PC-1, Japan’s first university-built stored-program computer, which became the nation’s fastest in 1958. Its low cost and stability shaped Japan’s early computer development and helped train its first generation of computer engineers.
