Panic of 1873

Its causes included rampant speculative investments, especially in railroads, the demonetization of silver, and economic disruptions from the Franco-Prussian War. The crisis began with the Vienna Stock Exchange crash in May 1873 and spread to North America as bank reserves plummeted.
