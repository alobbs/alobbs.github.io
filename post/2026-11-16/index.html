Designing a reliable unplanned failover

Neki’s Admin cannot determine from its own health check whether a Postgres primary has failed or is only unreachable, so unplanned failover requires corroborating evidence. Neki queries replicas and classifies the primary as dead only when Admin cannot reach it and at least one reachable replica reports broken replication with no contradicting replica evidence.
