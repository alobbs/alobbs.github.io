DKIM2 – Every system that handles an email signs for what it did

DKIM2 is an IETF draft email authentication scheme that aims to make forwarding, mailing lists, Bcc, and bounces verifiable by signing the SMTP envelope, recording every change, and supporting multiple algorithms at once. It addresses current gaps around proving the intended recipient, safe bounce handling, transit modifications, and crypto upgrades, providing accountability rather than trust. The specification is still active and not yet a finished standard, with several related drafts and early deployments.
