Dogs Seem to Agree with Humans That ‘Bouba’ Is Round and ‘Kiki’ Is Spiky

A new study found that domestic dogs, like humans, tend to associate the made-up sound “Bouba” with rounded shapes and “Kiki” with spiky shapes. In two experiments with 70 dogs, researchers tracked the animals’ gaze and found they looked more at rounded shapes during “Bouba” and spiky shapes during “Kiki,” even when owners wore headphones to prevent cues. The findings suggest dogs may share a cross-sensory bias linking sounds and shapes, though more research is needed.
