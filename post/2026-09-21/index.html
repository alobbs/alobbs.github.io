Why back propagation goes backward

The article explains why backpropagation uses a backward pass to compute neural network gradients: the chain rule requires information from downstream nodes that is only available after the forward pass has reached them. While derivatives can be computed locally, a purely forward algorithm cannot calculate weight gradients before all dependent downstream values and derivatives exist. Backward propagation therefore efficiently reuses intermediate terms to compute gradients for every weight.
