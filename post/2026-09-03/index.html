Officials note that pedestrian mortality is below 1% when vehicles travel at 30 km/h or slower, compared with much higher rates at higher speeds.

Kakistocracy

Kakistocracy is government by the worst, least qualified, or most unscrupulous people, a term coined in the seventeenth century from Greek words meaning “rule by the worst.” Historically, it has been used to describe governments such as Robespierre’s France, and in modern times it is applied critically to national governments including Russia, the Philippines, Brazil, and the Trump administration in the United States.

G20 nations unanimously endorse nonbinding US-proposed Carolina Principles for lighter AI regulation

All G20 nations unanimously endorsed the US-proposed Carolina Principles, a non-binding framework advocating a lighter-touch approach to regulating artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick announced the endorsement at a tech summit in North Carolina, marking a win for the Trump administration and Silicon Valley.
