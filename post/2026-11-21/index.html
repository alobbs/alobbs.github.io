How to generate an accessible color palette from a single color

Pablo Di Filippo describes a method for generating accessible color scales from a single color by assigning each step a target contrast ratio against the background and then computing the OKLCH lightness that meets it, as used in the newly MIT-licensed TaishoDS Core. He says an earlier approach based on evenly spaced lightness failed, and testing 4,096 starting colors in light and dark mode produced 81,920 steps whose text contrast never fell below 4.63, above WCAG AA’s 4.5. The article also notes exceptions such as yellow hues and cases the method does not cover.

Studios sometimes avoid such collisions by delaying, merging, or abandoning projects. Notable examples include competing adaptations and biopics released around the same period.
