Earth Stays Above 2°C of Warming for Six Days in a Row

For six consecutive days in late November, global average surface temperatures exceeded 2°C above the preindustrial baseline, the first such run on record, according to Copernicus ERA5 data. The streak peaked on 23 November at a record 2.24°C above the 1850–1900 average. Scientists cautioned that these daily readings do not mean the world has breached the Paris Agreement’s long-term temperature limits.

Stigler Diet

The Stigler diet is an optimization problem posed by economist George Stigler asking how a 154-pound man could meet nine 1943 recommended nutrient allowances at minimal cost using 77 foods. His solution used only wheat flour, evaporated milk, cabbage, spinach, and dried navy beans, costing about $0.11 a day in 1939 dollars. Though criticized for poor variety and palatability, the work is praised as an early application of linear programming.
