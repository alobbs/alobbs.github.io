Fixing GRPO’s credit assignment problem without evaluating every step

Researchers introduce ProVer, a framework that improves credit assignment in GRPO for agentic reinforcement learning by using an agentic judge to identify potentially pivotal trajectory segments and then verifying them through terminal success-rate differences between continuations before and after the segment. Verified positive estimates are incorporated into the GRPO advantages for tokens in that segment, avoiding exhaustive evaluation of every intermediate state. ProVer achieves the strongest average performance on ALFWorld, WebShop, and SearchQA, improving over GRPO by 9.91% and 7.12% for Qwen3.5-2B and Qwen3.5-4B, respectively.
