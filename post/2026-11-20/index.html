Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI

AI systems autonomously designed and verified Frostbite, a new general-purpose CPU architecture with a new instruction set built on interaction nets, iterating across compiler, ISA, microarchitecture, RTL, verification, and final GDS. The work demonstrates AI-driven hardware co-design can discover novel architectural primitives and deliver step-function gains in performance and area efficiency.
