Harness Engineering 101: The LLM is the brain, the harness is the body

“Harness Engineering 101” is a blog series published as a book that teaches building AI agents from first principles. Strot – Compile an LLM’s intent once, run decisions locally forever

Strot is an open-source decision compiler that uses an LLM once at compile time to turn intent and schema into a frozen Python artifact. At runtime, it performs classification, scoring, routing, and typed decisions locally on CPU at zero marginal cost, returning type-safe JSON with probabilities and confidence while abstaining on low-confidence records. It is installable via pip as strot-ai and licensed MIT.

The substitution myth & the tough spot it puts us in

The article argues that AI adoption rests on the “substitution myth”—the false assumption that AI can replace human work one-for-one without changing the broader work system. Instead, AI reshuffles roles and creates new tasks such as configuration, integration, and monitoring, making human cognitive work more critical even as adoption outpaces understanding.
