Study: AI mostly boosts output, not cuts costs; 80% AI-built apps automate manual tasks (Anthropic)

A study of 66 interviews with 58 Anthropic enterprise customers found that most AI deployments create value by expanding and accelerating output rather than reducing costs, including through coding agents used by nontechnical roles and AI-built apps that automate manual tasks. Firms reported few domestic layoffs driven by AI, but many said they are hiring less and reducing contractor use, especially offshore. Key bottlenecks include expert resistance, uneven adoption, and tasks such as code review and sales that do not speed up as fast as coding; the highest-value uses involved reorganizing workflows around AI.
