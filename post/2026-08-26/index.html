mold: A Massively Parallel Linker

mold is a Unix/Linux linker that applies data parallelism across the entire linking pipeline, decoupling symbol resolution and archive processing to overcome scalability limits of existing linkers. It links multi-gigabyte debug binaries in at most a few seconds, often under one second, making it 2.4–16.1x faster than lld and up to 112x faster than GNU ld. The speedup comes from the cumulative effect of parallelizing all passes rather than any single optimization.
